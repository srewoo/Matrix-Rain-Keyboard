# Privacy Policy — Digital Rain Keyboard

_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

Digital Rain Keyboard ("the extension") is built to respect your privacy.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any
personal data or browsing information.

## How keystrokes are used
- The extension notices when you press a key **only** to start an on-screen
  animation. If "Show typed characters" is on, the character you typed is drawn
  briefly in the animation on your own screen.
- Keystrokes are never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. They are used in the
  moment and discarded.
- Password, one-time-code and payment-card fields never display typed characters.
- In embedded frames (for example, a rich-text editor inside a page), the
  extension passes only the fact that a key was pressed — never which key — to
  its own code in the main page, so the animation can be drawn there.

## Data stored on your device
Your display settings (on/off, theme, speed, density, glyph size, dimming, and
the sites you turned it off for) are saved with `chrome.storage.local` in your
browser. They never leave your device.

## Network
The extension makes **no network requests** and contacts **no servers**.

## Permissions
- `storage`: to remember your display settings.
- Host access (`<all_urls>`): so the animation can be drawn on any page you type on.

## Third parties
There are no third-party services, analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs.
