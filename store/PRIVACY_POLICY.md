# Privacy Policy — Matrix Rain Keyboard

_Last updated: 2026-06-16_

Matrix Rain Keyboard ("the extension") is built to respect your privacy.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any
personal data or browsing information.

## Data handling
- The extension reads your keystrokes **only** to trigger an on-screen
  animation. Keystroke data is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere — it
  is used in the moment and discarded.
- A single on/off preference is saved locally via `chrome.storage.local` on
  your own device. It never leaves your browser.
- The extension makes **no network requests** and contacts **no servers**.

## Permissions
- `activeTab` / `scripting`: to draw the animation on the page you are viewing.
- `storage`: to remember whether the effect is enabled.
- Host access (`<all_urls>`): so the effect can run on any site you choose.

## Third parties
There are no third-party services, analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs.
